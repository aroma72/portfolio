The feature learners notice

The video stops and makes you think.

Every lesson carries a checkpoint question. It is never drawn or spoken in the video. The player pauses, the LMS asks, and you can't skip past it. Press play and try it.

War stories

The hardest bugs reported success.

The worst failures in an AI pipeline don't crash. They say "done" without doing the work. These are the ones I hunted down. Tap a card to see the fix.

The one rule underneath all of it
Encode correctness in structure.
Never just request it.
Every "please be careful" version of a fix measurably failed. Every structural one held.
"Please don't leave scenes blank."
→ A validator that rejects the shape.
"Please don't overspend."
→ A gate that runs before the money.
"Please don't touch the other beats."
→ A patch that's merged in code.
